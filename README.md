## 🧑‍💻 About Me

IT Engineer with **16+ years** in IT, including **8 years as Senior** and **1 year as Expert IT Engineer**, working remotely with European clients from **Germany** and **Norway** through outsourcing.

I focus on **Windows infrastructure**, **VMware VDI environments** and **automating everything that can be automated with PowerShell**. My work ranges from Group Policy design for VDI clients to deploying complex multi-tier software (server components, databases and clients).

- 🏢 Onboarding clients' environments: GPO for VDI clients, software deployment and licensing
- 🤖 Building automated software installation tooling (lightweight alternatives to winget / Chocolatey / Evergreen)
- 👨‍🏫 Former **PowerShell trainer**: taught 10 groups (100+ engineers)
- 👥 Led an onboarding team of 2–3 engineers, mentored new colleagues
- 🤝 Communicate directly with customers
- 🌱 Currently learning: **AI**, **GitHub Actions**, **Python** and **German** 🇩🇪
